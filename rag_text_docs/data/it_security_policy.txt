IT Security Policy - Northwind Software Inc.

All employees must enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) on their
company email, VPN, and source control accounts within their first week of
employment. Passwords must be at least 14 characters long, unique to each
system, and stored only in the company-approved password manager.

Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled and automatically
lock after 5 minutes of inactivity. Installing unapproved software or
browser extensions on company devices requires prior approval from the IT
Security team.

Employees must report any suspected phishing email, lost device, or
security incident to security@northwind.example within one hour of
discovery. Devices that are lost or stolen must be reported immediately so
IT can remotely wipe company data.

Access to production systems and customer data is granted on a
least-privilege basis and is reviewed quarterly. Employees who no longer
require access to a system, due to a role change or offboarding, will have
that access revoked within 24 hours.
