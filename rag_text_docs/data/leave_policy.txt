Leave and Time-Off Policy - Northwind Software Inc.

Full-time employees accrue 18 days of paid time off (PTO) per year, credited
monthly at a rate of 1.5 days per month. Unused PTO of up to 10 days may be
carried over into the following calendar year; any remaining balance beyond
that is forfeited on December 31st.

Employees are entitled to 10 paid sick days per year, which do not carry
over and are not paid out upon termination. A doctor's note is required for
sick leave lasting more than three consecutive days.

New parents, including adoptive and foster parents, are eligible for 16
weeks of paid parental leave. Parental leave must be taken within 12 months
of the birth, adoption, or foster placement, and can be split into two
blocks of at least two weeks each with manager approval.

Employees who wish to take a leave of absence for personal reasons beyond
their PTO balance may apply for unpaid leave of up to 30 days, subject to
approval from HR and their department head. All leave requests should be
submitted through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance, except in
cases of emergency.
