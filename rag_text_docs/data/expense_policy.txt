Expense Reimbursement Policy - Northwind Software Inc.

Employees may submit expense reports for business-related travel, client
meals, and approved software subscriptions. All expense reports must be
submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred, along with an
itemized receipt for any single expense over $25.

Airfare must be booked in economy class for flights under six hours;
business class is permitted for flights longer than six hours with prior
manager approval. Ground transportation, including taxis and ride-share
services, is reimbursable when used for business travel or when working
after 8:00 PM on a company-required task.

Client meal expenses are reimbursable up to $75 per person, and alcohol is
reimbursable only when it accompanies a client meal and does not exceed 20%
of the total bill. Personal expenses, such as movies, mini-bar charges, and
gym fees, are not reimbursable.

Expense reports are submitted through the finance portal and are typically
reimbursed within 10 business days of manager approval. Reports missing
receipts or exceeding policy limits will be returned for correction.
