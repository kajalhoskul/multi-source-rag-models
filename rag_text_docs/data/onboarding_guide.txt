New Hire Onboarding Guide - Northwind Software Inc.

Before your start date, HR will send you a welcome email containing your
employee ID, start date, and instructions for setting up your company
laptop. Your manager will assign you an onboarding buddy from your team who
will help you navigate your first two weeks.

On your first day, you should report to the front desk at 9:00 AM to
receive your access badge and complete I-9 verification. IT will meet you
within the first hour to set up your accounts, including email, Slack, and
the internal wiki.

During your first week, you are expected to complete mandatory compliance
training covering IT security, workplace conduct, and data privacy. This
training must be finished within 5 business days of your start date and is
tracked in the HR learning portal.

Your 30-60-90 day plan will be created with your manager during your first
week. At the 90-day mark, you will have a formal check-in with your manager
and HR to review your progress and set goals for the next quarter.
