Remote Work Policy - Northwind Software Inc.

All full-time employees are eligible to work remotely up to three days per
week, subject to manager approval. Employees who wish to work remotely on a
permanent, full-time basis must submit a Remote Work Request Form to HR and
receive written approval from their department head.

Employees working remotely are expected to be reachable during core hours,
which are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM in their local time zone. Remote employees
must attend all scheduled team meetings via video conference with their
camera on, unless otherwise agreed with their manager.

The company will reimburse up to $500 per year for home office equipment,
including desks, chairs, and monitors, upon submission of receipts to the
finance team. Internet and phone stipends of $50 per month are available to
employees who work remotely more than two days per week.

Remote employees must use company-issued laptops and connect to internal
systems only through the corporate VPN. Working from a public location such
as a cafe is permitted only for non-confidential tasks and never while
accessing customer data.
